Pitwall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Pitwall is a scouting app for FIRST Robotics Competition teams, made by FRC Team 3925, Ventura Robotics. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Your name as you enter it, so teammates can see who scouted what. No email is needed.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to sign in with email instead of a name-only account.
  • Your team membership and roles (for example scout or lead).
  • Scouting data you submit: match reports, pit reports, notes, and photos of robots.
  • A push notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications, so leads can send you scouting reminders.
  • Records of team actions (an audit log, such as who approved a member), visible to your team's leads.

Pitwall does not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. It has no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics.

Who can see it

Your data is visible only to members of your own team, according to their roles. Other teams cannot see it. Public FRC information (team numbers, names and match schedules) comes from The Blue Alliance.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Push notifications are delivered through Expo, Apple and Google. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or photo library only when you add a robot photo during pit scouting. Photos are uploaded to your team only. Teams can delete old photos at any time.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: More → Delete account. This permanently deletes your login and profile. Scouting reports you submitted stay with your team but no longer show your name. See how to delete your account.

Students

Pitwall is used by student robotics teams. We ask only for a name (which can be a first name or nickname) and collect nothing beyond what scouting needs.
