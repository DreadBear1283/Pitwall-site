Delete your Pitwall account

In the app

  1. Open Pitwall and go to the More tab.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. If you are your team's only admin, make someone else an admin first.

Without the app

Email chandlerwiese@gmail.com with your name and team number. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, name, email (if any), roles, and push notification tokens: deleted permanently.
  • Scouting reports, notes and robot photos you submitted: kept for your team, with your name removed.

See the privacy policy for details.
